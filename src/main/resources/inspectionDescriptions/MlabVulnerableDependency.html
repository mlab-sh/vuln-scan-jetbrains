Reports dependencies with known vulnerabilities in a lockfile, from its last mlab scan.

One problem is reported per advisory, on the line that declares the package. Critical and high findings are errors, medium findings warnings and low or unrated findings weak warnings; anything below the severity floor set in Settings | Tools | mlab is reported as a weak warning.

This inspection only reads results already stored on your machine. It never uploads anything: scans are started with Check for Lock Vulnerabilities or, once you have agreed, automatically when a lockfile changes.

Supported lockfiles: Cargo.lock, package-lock.json, npm-shrinkwrap.json, composer.lock, Gemfile.lock, go.sum, requirements.txt, mise.lock.
